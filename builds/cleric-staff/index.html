Why this way

  1. Energy Bolt 20 is damage 130 on one mob for 16 MP. Magic Claw 20 is the same 130 split into two 65 hits for 20 MP and less range, 300 against 350, so Energy Bolt is the one to max.
  2. Magic Guard 1 goes in at 17: it's a toggle, and even 1 point sends 30% of the damage you take to MP instead of HP. Mage HP is small, so it pays off early.
  3. Improved MP Recovery 15 makes MP potions heal 25% more, so every potion goes 25% further. Max MP Increase 15 is +25% max MP.
  4. Teleport 1 and Heal 1 go in at 30 and 31: getting around, and a heal for you and your party.
  5. Holy Arrow 30 first: damage 160 on one mob against Energy Bolt's 130, more on undead. In our damage engine it takes over from Energy Bolt by 40.
  6. Heal 30 next: it hits up to 6 undead mobs around you and heals your party. On Jr. Wraith maps it hits up to 6 Wraiths at once.
  7. MP Eater 20 after that: a 50% chance to drain MP from a mob, once every 5 seconds, so fewer MP potions.
  8. Invincible 5 opens Bless, and Bless 20 cuts the damage your whole party takes for 300 seconds. The last 15 points take Invincible to 20, 20% less physical damage for you.
  9. LUK only goes in when your gear needs it: 10 for Emerald Staff at 15, 15 for Old Wooden Staff at 20, 20 for Wizard Staff at 25, 25 for level 35 magician armor, 30 for level 40 magician armor and 40 for Arc Staff at 45. Everything else goes into INT.
  10. Staffs have no step between Wizard Staff at 25, magic attack 45, and Arc Staff at 45, 73. A wand mage has 55 to 69 over those levels, so a staff mage spends 30 to 44 with 10 to 24 less magic attack, then pulls ahead from 45.

Common mistakes

  1. Maxing Magic Claw on top of Energy Bolt. Both are 130 at 20, so that's 20 SP for nothing, and Claw costs 20 MP a cast against 16. Put them in MP Recovery and Max MP.
  2. Leveling Heal first and training off undead maps. Heal only hurts undead; anywhere else Holy Arrow is your attack.
  3. Leaving MP Eater at 0. At 20 it's a 50% chance to drain MP from a mob every 5 seconds; without it you buy more MP potions.
  4. Forgetting LUK for Arc Staff. It needs 40 LUK at 45, so put your AP at 44 and 45 into LUK, or you stay on Wizard Staff's 45 magic attack instead of 73.
