Why this way

  1. Power Strike 20 hits one mob for 260% and Slash Blast 20 hits up to 6 for 130%. Max both first and by 23 you have a hit for one mob and a hit for groups.
  2. Precise Strikes 15 is +20 accuracy and +5% critical rate. The last 6 points of 1st job go into HP: Improved HP Recovery 5, then Max HP Increase 1, which needs it at 3 and is +10% max HP from its first point.
  3. Sword Mastery 5 opens Sword Booster, and Booster 1 already gives the full attack speed boost. Points 2 to 20 only make it last 300 seconds instead of 100 and cut its HP and MP cost from 30 to 10, so they wait.
  4. Final Attack: Sword 1 goes in at 32: when you use an attack skill there's a 25% chance it unlocks Final Attack for 60 seconds, and while it's lit you can use it on one mob for 270%. 1 point already gets the full chance.
  5. Sword Mastery 20 comes next. At 40 those 15 points raise your average hit about 28%, because mastery lifts your lowest hits, and they're +20 accuracy.
  6. Final Attack points 2 to 30 take it from 270% to 360%, so they come right after Mastery.
  7. Rush 1 at 33 is for getting around: it carries you forward and hits up to 6 mobs for 60%. Points 2 to 20 only raise that to 80%, so they come after Booster.
  8. Threaten goes last, with the 30 points nothing else needs by then: at 30 it lowers up to 4 mobs' weapon defense by 10 and slows them for 30 seconds.
  9. DEX only goes in when your gear needs it: 15 for Viking Sword at 20, 20 for Eloon at 25, 30 for Gladius at 30, 35 for Traus at 40, 40 for Hero's Gladius at 45 and 45 for Jeweled Katar at 50. Everything else goes into STR.
  10. A one-handed sword leaves room for a shield and its weapon defense, but at the same attack, with your stats at 40, it hits about 21% softer than a two-handed one. Some one-handed swords swing a speed step faster, which wins a little of that back. Pick it if you'd rather take less damage than kill faster.

Common mistakes

  1. Putting points into Iron Body. It needs Max HP Increase 3, so the 3 points come out of Precise Strikes: 3 less accuracy and 1% less critical rate, for +20% weapon defense you recast every 300 seconds.
  2. Maxing Sword Booster early. Points 2 to 20 only stretch it from 100 to 300 seconds. That's 19 SP, over 6 levels, that should be in Mastery and Final Attack.
  3. Putting Threaten ahead of Final Attack. -10 weapon defense on 4 mobs is about +8% damage on a Jr. Wraith, whose weapon defense drops from 30 to 20; Final Attack 2 to 30 takes it from 270% to 360%.
  4. Forgetting DEX before Viking Sword. It needs 15 DEX at 20, so put your AP at 19 and 20 into DEX on the levels up to it, or you stay on Saw's 36 attack instead of 37, and a speed step slower.
