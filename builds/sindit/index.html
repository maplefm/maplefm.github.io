Sindit: Lucky Seven to 44, then Savage Blow

Thief · Thief (10) → Bandit (30) · levels 10 to 100 · Medium

Throw Lucky Seven while you train, put Bandit points into the dagger skills, and switch to a dagger at 44. No reset needed.

Best at
  1. Safe ranged training 10 to 43
  2. Hits monsters on other platforms
  3. Big single target damage from 44
  4. No Cash Shop needed
Weak spots
  1. Bandit points do nothing until you switch
  2. Melee from 44: takes hits
  3. Stars cost mesos until 44
  4. DEX for daggers: 45 by 50, 65 by 70
Gear to 50

about 539K

claws and daggers to 50 at live typical prices; stars extra

Why this way

  1. Throw while you train: Lucky Seven hits from 200 to 400 away, so you pick monsters off a platform without walking up or taking touch damage
  2. Switch at 44, not 35: a dagger only wins on one monster standing still before then. Once Savage Blow has 21 points and mastery is 20, it wins by enough to cover the walking
  3. Dagger points first: Bandit points can't help a claw, so they all go toward the switch and it lands as early as possible
  4. Korean Fan: speed 3 and no DEX need, so the switch costs no AP
  5. No reset: Lucky Seven's 20 points stay in the book. Nothing is lost by leaving them

Common mistakes

  1. Switching to a dagger at 35: melee before Savage Blow 21 trains slower than throwing
  2. Haste before the dagger skills: it pushes the switch back several levels
  3. More than 1 point in Dagger Booster early: level 1 already gives the full speed up
  4. Skipping DEX before 50: Shinkita needs 45 DEX, Deadly Fin 55, Kandine 65
  5. Buying Kumbi or Tobi stars: you stop throwing at 44
