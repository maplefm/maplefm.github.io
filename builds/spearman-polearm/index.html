Spearman, polearm

Warrior · Warrior (10) → Spearman (30) · levels 10 to 100 · Easy

Swing-heavy spearman: the most damage per hit a 2nd job warrior gets.

Best at
  1. Highest Power Strike damage
  2. Mobbing with Slash Blast and Rush
  3. Fast walking (+20 speed)
Weak spots
  1. Accuracy at 30 to 40 (Jr. Wraiths, Lupins)
  2. Potion cost
  3. Big gap between low and high rolls
  4. Slowest weapons (speed 8)

Why this way

  1. Polearm or spear: polearm swings at 3.5x and stabs at 1.5x, spear the reverse; most warrior hits are swings
  2. Precise Strikes 15: +20 accuracy and +5% crit (game files); missing is the warrior's weak spot from 30 to 40
  3. DEX only for weapons: each spear and polearm from 30 needs DEX equal to its level; Bamboo Spear and the Mops need none
  4. Booster at 1 point: the game files give the full 2-step speed up at level 1
  5. Final Attack 30 before Booster 20: 350% hit in the game files vs 260% Power Strike
  6. Polearm swings at 3.5x: 60% of warrior attacks are swings, so the average hit is higher

Common mistakes

  1. Skipping DEX: Forked Spear and Mithril Pole Arm need 30 DEX at 30, 70 by 70
  2. Booster past 1 before mastery: extra points only lengthen the buff
  3. Iron Will early: defense only; last in the order
  4. AP Reset to fix skills: SP and AP resets are different scrolls
  5. Maxing both weapon lines: not enough points; pick one
