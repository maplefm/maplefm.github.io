Why this way

  1. Lucky Seven throws 2 stars for 140% each at 20. Max it first and you hit from range from level 10.
  2. Keen Eyes 15 adds range to Lucky Seven, 200 at 15, and needs Nimble Body 3. Nimble Body 15 is +15 accuracy and +15 avoid.
  3. Disorder 3 opens Dark Sight, and the last points go there.
  4. This plan is the claw start most thieves use. If you want a dagger and Double Stab from 10, the Pure Bandit and STR Bandit guides start that way from level 1.
  5. DEX only goes in when your gear needs it: 15 for Mithril Titans at 15, 20 for Adamantium Igor at 20, 25 for Meba at 25, 30 for Adamantium Guards at 30 and 40 for level 50 thief armor. Everything else goes into LUK.

Common mistakes

  1. Buying Kumbi or Tobi stars to level. Kumbi is +4 attack over Wolbi, 21 against 17, for a lot more mesos. Wolbi from Max in Kerning City is all you need.
