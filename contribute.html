Setup, about five minutes

  1. Download and unzip FMWatcher.zip.
  2. Double-click install.bat. It installs Python and Tesseract through Windows' own installer. Close it at Done.
  3. Open MapleStory, then double-click start.bat and leave it open. It reads your IGN off screen for the ranks.
  4. Leave the black window where you can glance at it. It says DEAL when a shop you open has something well under the usual price, and names the rooms and channels nobody has read at this hour.
  5. Optional: run snapshot.bat with a shop open to save a picture of what it reads in review.

While you shop

Open the Change Channel window once every login. Without it, your scans can't be placed on a channel and FM room.
  1. Keep the minimap in view. It tells us which FM room you're in.
  2. Scroll each shop to the bottom, about a second per scroll.

Reading equip stats

Gear is priced by stats. First to capture an equip's stats earns a tenth of a credit.

  1. Hover the item's icon for about a second, until the chime and the watcher says read.
  2. Keep the whole popup on screen. If it runs off the bottom, scroll the item higher first.
  3. Equips only. Scrolls, potions and etc items have no stats to read.
  4. Trade chat links count. Click a linked item and its stats attach to the post.

Your prices show on the site within about 15 minutes.

    All the rules

      Flags don't block anyone; they mark weeks for review before approval. Confirmed cheating zeroes the week and locks the account.

      What it does, and what it never does

      It only reads your screen

      • Looks at the shop windows and chat box, the same pixels you see.
      • Reads item names and prices off them.
      • Sends only what's in the shop: item, price, quantity, equip stats, the shop owner's name as the FM shows it, the room, channel and time, plus a daily count of hovers read and missed.
      • For items listed at 500K and up, a small picture of the item's popup, so buyers see the real stats. Never your screen.
      • Up to 30 small pictures a day of a popup or room name it couldn't read, so we can fix the reader. Just that crop, never your screen.
      • Puts you on the site's leaderboard under the name you choose, or as Guest.

      It never touches your game

      • Never clicks, types or presses a key in MapleStory.
      • Runs no macros, prompts or scripts in the game.
      • Never takes control of your client or your computer.
      • Never reads or changes game files or game memory, and never reads network traffic.
      • Never uploads screenshots of your screen. Your review folder stays on your PC.

      Plain Python you can read in Notepad. It does only what writing prices down by hand would. Using it is your choice and your responsibility.

      Safe to run

      1. Nothing hidden. No .exe. Plain Python files you can read in Notepad, full source on GitHub.
      2. Official installers only. Python and Tesseract through Windows' own installer, or from python.org and Tesseract's official releases. Nothing else.
      3. Stays in its folder. Never opens your other files, browsers, passwords or game files.
      4. No login needed to scan. Prices go up under a random ID and your chosen name, or Guest. Discord sign in is only for pay.
      5. Updates are locked down. Only from this site, each checked against its fingerprint before it installs. Only the Club FM admin can publish one.
      6. Easy to remove. Close the black window and delete the folder.

      "Windows protected your PC"? Windows shows this for any script downloaded from the internet that isn't from a big publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway. It shows once per file. Unsure? Open the .bat in Notepad first: it's a few lines.

      Can I get banned?

      1. It reads pixels, like OBS or a screenshot tool. Nothing is sent to the game, and nothing in the game is read but what's on your screen.
      2. No clicking, no keys, no automation. You play by hand. The watcher only watches.
      3. We can't speak for Nexon. It's built to stay on the safe side: screen reading only. Running it is your call.

      Updating

      start.bat patches itself each time you open it, like a game launcher. See the purple update banner or a new version notice? Close the watcher and open start.bat again. Settings and saved prices are kept.

      Copy from before October 19? Download it once more and copy your config.json into the new folder. It updates itself from then on.
