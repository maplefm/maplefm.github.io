How to price an item

MapleStory Classic guide · updated October 30, 2026

Prices on Club FM come from real Free Market sales that players watched happen, not from what sellers hope to get.

Five steps

  1. Search it. Type the name on the price board. Short names work, like GFA 60 or wg. The full list is on shortcuts.
  2. Read Typical. It's the middle of real sales from the last 24 hours, or the last 3 days when it's quiet. If the card says "from asks", no sales were caught yet and it's based on asking prices, so treat it as a rough guide.
  3. Check the range and the trend. The range is the low and high of recent sales. The 24h and 7 day moves tell you if the price is climbing or falling.
  4. Look at what's for sale. Cheapest now and Where to buy show the listings you'd be competing with, with the seller, channel and room.
  5. Set your price. Just under the cheapest listing sells fastest. Never go under the low end of recent sales, and never under what an NPC pays.
Example, October 30, 2026: Gloves Attack Scroll 60% has a typical of 930K, a range of 810K to 950K, from 85 watched sales. Listing well above 950K usually sits unsold.

Equips and scrolls

  • Equips sell by stats. A clean pair of Work Gloves and a 3 attack pair are different items. Open Price by stat on the item card to see what each roll sells for.
  • Scrolls sell by success rate. Cheapest per success on a scroll's card shows which rate gives you the most successes for your mesos.

Before a big trade

  • Check the date. A typical with few sales can move fast. The card says how many sales back it up.
  • Hover the listing. In game, check the stats yourself before paying a roll price.
