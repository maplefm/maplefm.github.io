How to read the board

MapleStory Classic guide · updated October 30, 2026

The price board lists every item players have caught for sale. Tap any row for its item card.

The columns

  • Typical: the middle of recent real sales. Asking prices are used only when no sales were caught yet.
  • 24h: today's typical against the 24 hours before.
  • Cheapest now: the lowest listing seen in the last 24 hours that nobody has seen sold or gone since.
  • Trend: the typical price over the last 7 days.
  • Sales: how many sales were caught.
  • Volume: how many mesos changed hands, so you can see what's actually busy.

The tabs

  • Board: every item, searchable, with category and class filters.
  • Best deals: listings well under their typical. Public deals show 10 minutes after they're caught; Mega Deals members see them live.
  • Sell my items: a list of what you want to sell, with a suggested price for each.
  • My positions: log what you buy and sell to track your profit.

The item card

  • Where to buy: every listing still up, cheapest first, with seller, channel, room and when it was seen. Listings over 2 hours old are dimmed.
  • Recent sales: what sold, for how much and when.
  • Price by stat: on equips, what each roll sells for.
  • Check mark: a second scanner or a stream saw the same sale.
  • Report a wrong price: at the bottom of every card, if something looks off.
