How Club FM works

Under the hood.

Where prices come from

  1. Players in the Free Market. Volunteers run FM Watcher, a free Windows program, while they shop. It reads item names, prices, quantities and sold slots off their screen, and equip stats on hover. The browser scanner runs the same readers in a browser tab.
  2. Trade chat and chalkboards. Chat posts and Free Market Entrance chalkboards count as asking prices.
  3. Public Twitch streams. Shops shown on MapleStory Classic streams are read the same way and credited to the channel.

Only what any player can see in game. No hacking, packet reading or access to Nexon's servers.

How a price is set

  1. Typical price: the middle of recent real sales, slots that sold out between two scans. Asking prices only when there are no sales yet.
  2. Equips are priced by roll. 10 attack Work Gloves and a clean pair are each judged against sales with the same stats and slots.
  3. Filtered: outliers and misreads. A check mark means a second scanner or a stream saw the same sale.
  4. Refresh: about every 15 minutes. Prices can be wrong or late, so check before a big trade.

What we collect

  1. Game screen: items, prices, stats, the shop owner's in-game name, channel, room and time. Nothing else leaves a scanner's PC.
  2. Visitors: a visit count only. Each view logs the page, referring site and day, with a scrambled daily ID so you count once a day, and whether this browser has visited before (the first day is kept in your browser only). No cookies, no account, no stored IP. Watchlist and settings stay in your browser.
  3. !price command: only the item name typed. We never see who asked.
  4. Contributors: Discord sign in, used for ranks and payouts only.

Full legal wording is in the Terms of Use.

Safety

  1. Viewers: the board is a public web page. No downloads, no logins, no ads.
  2. Streamers: !price only fetches a line of text, like !weather. It can't post on its own or touch channel settings.
  3. Scanners: FM Watcher only reads the screen, like streaming software. It never clicks, types, moves your character, or touches game files or memory. Club FM isn't approved by Nexon, so we can't promise zero risk, but the watcher does nothing to the game.
  4. Game rules: Club FM never buys or sells mesos, items, accounts or access for real money.

Who runs it

  1. Built by one player, run for the community, not for profit. Costs paid out of pocket. No investors, ads or outside owners.
  2. Ko-fi donations go to the players who scan, through Earn by scanning.
  3. Some streamers are sponsored, and sponsored streams say so.
  4. Questions or ideas? Ask in the Club FM Discord.
