How Club FM works

The full picture, for anyone who wants to look under the hood.

Where prices come from

  1. Players in the Free Market. Volunteers run FM Watcher, a free Windows program, while they shop. It reads the shop windows on their own screen: item names, prices, quantities and sold slots. Resting the mouse on an equip lets it read the item's stats.
  2. Trade chat and chalkboards. Buy and sell posts seen in chat, and chalkboards in the Free Market Entrance, count as asking prices.
  3. Public Twitch streams. When a MapleStory Classic stream shows Free Market shops, those shops are read the same way and credited to the channel.

Everything comes from what any player can see in game. There is no hacking, packet reading or access to Nexon's servers.

How a price is set

  1. The typical price is the middle of recent real sales, a slot that sold out between two scans. Asking prices are only used when there are no sales yet.
  2. Equips are priced by their roll. A 10 attack Work Gloves and a clean pair are judged against sales of the same stats and slots used.
  3. Odd outliers and misreads are filtered out, and a check mark means a second scanner or a stream saw the same sale.
  4. Prices refresh about every 15 minutes. They can still be wrong or late, so check before a big trade.

What we collect

  1. From the game screen: items, prices, stats, the shop owner's in-game name as shown in the Free Market, channel, room and time. Nothing else on a scanner's screen leaves their PC.
  2. From visitors: a visit count only. Each page view records the page, the site you came from and the day, with a scrambled daily ID so one visitor counts once a day. No cookies, no account, and your IP address is never stored. Your watchlist and settings stay in your browser.
  3. From the !price chat command: only the item name typed. We never see who asked.
  4. Contributors who want credit sign in with Discord. That's used for ranks and payouts only.

Full legal wording is in the Terms of Use.

Safety

  1. Viewers: the board is a public web page. No downloads, no logins, no ads.
  2. Streamers: the !price command only fetches a line of text, like any !weather command. It can't post on its own or touch your channel settings.
  3. Scanners: FM Watcher only reads the screen, the same way streaming software captures it. It never clicks, types, moves your character, or touches the game's files or memory. Club FM isn't approved by Nexon, so we can't promise zero risk, but the watcher does nothing to the game.
  4. Game rules: Club FM never buys or sells mesos, items, accounts or access for real money.

Who runs it

Club FM is built by one player who loves the game, and it's run for the community, not for profit. The developer pays the costs out of pocket. There are no investors, no ads and no outside owners.

Donations on Ko-fi go back to the players who scan prices, through the Earn by scanning program. The goal is a site that pays for itself and answers only to its players.

Some streamers who use Club FM on stream are sponsored, and sponsored streams say so.
