Guild FM Prices

Going rates in the MapleStory Classic Free Market, built from shop scans and priced sell messages in chat. The going rate is the median asking price per unit, which ignores joke listings. Average counts every listing. Sold at is the median of listings that sold out. An item shows up once it has been seen in at least 5 listings. Updated after each scan.
