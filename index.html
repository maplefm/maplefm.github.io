About this site

This page shows what items really sell for in the MapleStory Classic Free Market, read off the screen by our guild's FM Watcher while we shop. Pick an item to see its price chart, its recent sales and where to buy it. It updates itself every minute.

Prices are real sales. A sale is a shop row seen greyed out, which means someone paid the price printed on it. Asking prices of rows still for sale are shown apart and are never counted as sales.

Typical is the middle price of an item's recent sales: the last 24 hours when 5 or more sold in them, otherwise its last 10 sales. The typical range is the middle half of those same sales. Items with fewer than 3 sales fall back to asking prices and say Ask, and items seen fewer than 5 times say Low data.

24h is the typical sale of the last 24 hours against the 24 hours before it. Cheapest now is the lowest listing still up from the last day. In the item panel, a listing is likely still up when it was seen in the last 3 hours, and crossed out when the same shop was seen again later without it.

The red banner lists every listing seen in the last 10 minutes at half or less of what the item sells for, or 30% under for items worth 10,000 or more, newest first.

Sell my items prices what you're holding. My positions tracks what you bought to flip, and the star in an item's panel watches it; both ping you when the price moves past your set percent in a day. They're saved in this browser only.

On the chart, filled dots are sales, hollow dots are asking prices, the line is the daily median and the shaded band is the typical range. Press / to search. Guild members' own shops are left out.

Where prices come from

How the prices are read

Every price comes from guildmates running FM Watcher while they play, and from public MapleStory Classic streams our Stream Reader watches. The program finds Free Market shop windows on screen, reads each row's item and price, and checks them against consistency rules before anything is published.

A sale is a shop row seen greyed out, or a row whose stock dropped between visits, at the price printed on it. Coverage depends on where we walked, so some items only have a few sales.

Streamers who would rather be left out can tell Adam and their channel is skipped from then on.

No footage is stored or hosted. Seller names are the shop owners' in-game names, as anyone in the Free Market could see them.

Item details, icons, levels and classes come from maplestory.io.

Thanks to the people scanning

Record your shopping

Walk the Free Market with FM Watcher running and it reads the shops for you. Every shop you open adds sales and listings here within a few minutes.

Get set up

  1. Ask Adam for a watcher slot and the FM Watcher download for your name.
  2. Make your own GitHub token with the steps in the README. Never send it to anyone.
  3. Double-click start.bat, then play as normal.

While you shop

  1. Open the channel menu once, or type your channel in channel.txt, so listings show the channel.
  2. Keep the minimap in view. It tells us which Free Market room you're in.
  3. Scroll each shop slowly to the bottom so every row is read.
  4. Keep the cursor off the item prices, except to hover an item for a second when you want its stats read.

Streaming instead?

If you stream MapleStory Classic on Twitch with Classic in your title, the Stream Reader can read your Free Market trips too. Tell Adam if you'd rather be left out.
