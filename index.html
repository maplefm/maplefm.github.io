How this works

Our guild's FM Watcher programs read Free Market shops and trade chat off the screen while we play, and this page updates itself every minute.

Typical is the median of real sales: shop rows seen greyed out, which means someone paid that price. Items with fewer than 3 sales fall back to the median asking price and are marked ASK. Items seen fewer than 5 times are marked LOW DATA.

Cheapest now is the lowest listing still for sale seen in the last day, with the seller, channel and room. It may be gone by the time you get there. Listings seen in the last 3 hours are marked as likely still up.

The red banner lists every listing seen in the last 10 minutes at half or less of what the item sells for, newest first. Tick the sound box to get a beep when a new one comes in.

On the chart, filled dots are sales, hollow dots are asking prices, and the shaded band is the middle half of sales. Press / to search. Guild members' own shops are left out.
