Throwing stars and arrows

Every star, and the arrows for bows and crossbows, with attack, set size, where an NPC sells them and what they go for on the Free Market.

What to buy

Buy Subi and Wolbi from an NPC and recharge them there. Dr. Faymus in Kerning City sells Subi for 500 a set.

Everything past that comes from drops and the Free Market, and the price climbs fast: Mokbi about 228K, Kumbi about 787K, Tobi about 4M, Steely about 13.5M. They're a luxury for bossing or endgame, not part of any build, and no Club FM guide tells you to buy them.
