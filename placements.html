Free Market Shop Spots

Where to open your shop so the most shops fit in every room. Shops can't open too close to an up or down arrow, or too close to another shop. Packing them edge to edge from one end, instead of anywhere, fits more on every floor.

How it works

  1. Every Henesys room (FM 1 to 6) has the same layout, and so does every Perion room (FM 7 to 11). Only the arrows move a few pixels, and FM 4 and FM 11 have an extra exit.
  2. Stand on a spot and open your shop. Start at the left end of a floor and go right, one shop width at a time, and nobody's shop gets squeezed out.
  3. The gold spots sit right next to an arrow, where everyone moving between floors walks past.

Floors, ladders and arrows come from the game's own map data (maplestory.io, MapleStory Classic World). The spacing rule isn't published, so it's measured from real shops in stream frames and screenshots: the closest two shops we've seen were about 115 apart, and the closest shop to an arrow about 65 away. The top floor in a busy room really does hold the 8 shops shown here. If the game tells you "you can't open a shop here" on a spot, nudge the sliders and tell the guild.
