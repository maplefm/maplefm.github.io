Channel: open the Change Channel window once after you start.

  1. Press Start and pick the MapleStory window in the share list.
  2. Open shops in the Free Market and scroll to the bottom, like with FM Watcher.
  3. Open Change Channel once (Esc, then Change Channel) so your scans land on the right channel. Changing channels is picked up too.
  4. Hover equips to read their stats. Only popups read exactly are kept.
  5. Leave this tab open. It can sit behind the game.

Good to know

  • Browsers: Chrome or Edge on a computer work best. Firefox works. Safari and phones can't share a game window.
  • Game size: the default 1920 × 1080 window reads best. Share the game window, not the whole screen.
  • Speed: it reads only when the picture changes and waits between reads, so the game keeps its frame rate.
  • Earn: sign in with Discord and your scans join your account. FM Watcher and this page on one account count as one scanner.
  • Privacy: frames are read inside this tab and thrown away. Nothing is recorded or uploaded except the listings.
  • Problem? Tell us in Discord with your browser and game size.
