Club FMOpen the price board

Club FM for streamers

Your chat asks "what's this worth?" all stream. Give them an answer from real Free Market sales, priced by the item's roll, in one command.

!price in your chat

Viewers type !price claw 60 and your bot answers with the typical sale, the range, the cheapest listing up right now and today's move.

Priced by roll

A 10 attack Work Gloves and a clean pair aren't the same item. Equips are priced by their attack or main stat, and by slots used.

Your shops count

Every shop your stream shows on screen is read and credited to your channel on the board, and linking Twitch earns you credit in the Earn program.

Quest Log

Every quest at your level, what it needs, where it drops, and what it costs to just buy. Send chat to maplefm.gg/quests.html.

Add the Club FM bot

One click, no Nightbot needed

Sign in with the Twitch account you stream on and our bot answers !price, !pc and !fm in your chat as a reply to whoever asked. It only reads commands, keeps a 3 second cooldown, and you can remove it anytime under Twitch Settings, Connections.

Add the Club FM bot to my chat

Or use your own bot

Already run Nightbot, StreamElements or Fossabot? Paste one of these in your chat as a mod:

Nightbot

!commands add !price $(urlfetch https://clubfm-accounts.aaweaver93.workers.dev/chat/price?q=$(querystring))

StreamElements

!command add !price ${customapi.https://clubfm-accounts.aaweaver93.workers.dev/chat/price?q=${queryescape ${1:}}}

Fossabot: add a command !price with the response

$(customapi https://clubfm-accounts.aaweaver93.workers.dev/chat/price?q=$(querystring))

Get credit for your stream

  1. Stream MapleStory Classic as you normally do. When you open shops in the Free Market, Club FM reads them and credits your channel on the board under On stream.
  2. Sign in at maplefm.gg/contribute.html with Discord and link your Twitch. Your shops then count toward the Earn program.
  3. Join the Club FM Discord to get the Streamer role and a direct line for feature requests.

Partner streamers

We work with a few Classic streamers who use Club FM on stream: a partner spot on the board, a !price command set up for you, features built for your stream, and support for the channel. Message Maya in the Club FM Discord.
