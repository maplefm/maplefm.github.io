Add the Club FM bot to my chat

Sign in with Twitch on your streaming account and click Authorize. clubfmbot joins your chat and answers !price, !pc and !fm. Follower-only or slow mode? Type /mod clubfmbot. Remove it anytime under Twitch Settings, Connections.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Free, no ads, no sign up to look up prices.

Is it safe for my stream and my viewers?

Yes. clubfmbot only answers !price, !pc and !fm with one line, a few seconds apart at most. It never posts on its own, keeps no chat logs, and links only go to maplefm.gg.

Where do the prices come from?

Real sales seen in Free Market shops and trade chat by players who scan. Equips are priced by stats. Full details.

Who runs Club FM?

One player, not for profit, paid out of pocket. Donations go to the players who scan. More.
