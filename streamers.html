Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Club FM is free for everyone, with no ads and no sign-up needed to look up prices.

Is it safe for my stream and my viewers?

Yes. The command only fetches one line of text from our server, the same as any !weather or !uptime command. It can't post anything on its own, and it doesn't see who asked. Viewers who click a link just land on a public web page.

Where do the prices come from?

Players who shop in the Free Market and real sales seen in shops and trade chat. Equips are priced by their stats. Full details on how the data comes in.

Who runs Club FM?

A player who loves the game. It's community-run and not for profit, paid for out of pocket. Donations go back to the players who scan prices. More about the project.
