Terms of Use

Last updated October 21, 2026

Club FM (maplefm.gg) is a free, fan-made price board for the MapleStory Classic Free Market. The prices on it are gathered by players who run our FM Watcher while they shop, and from public MapleStory Classic streams. By using the site you agree to these terms.

The short version
  1. Browse all you like, and quote a price anywhere with a link back to maplefm.gg.
  2. Scanners keep their own scans. Everything you record stays on your PC too, and you can export it any time.
  3. No bots, scrapers or AI systems pulling the price tables or data files without asking us first.
  4. Prices can be wrong. The screen reader makes mistakes, and the market moves. Check before a big trade.

1. Using the site

You may use the site in a browser, as a person, to look up prices for your own play, and share links to it. You may quote a small number of prices elsewhere with a visible credit and link to maplefm.gg.

2. What needs permission first

Without written permission from us, you may not:

  1. Collect the price tables or data files by automated means, such as scripts, crawlers, headless browsers or direct bulk requests to the data files.
  2. Copy, mirror, republish or resell the price data or sales records, or build another site, app, bot or dataset from them.
  3. Use the site or its data to train or feed a machine learning or AI system.
  4. Work around rate limits or blocks, or load the site in a way that slows it for others.

Normal search engine indexing and link previews are fine. If you want to build on the data, ask. We like projects and usually say yes.

3. Contributing scans

FM Watcher only reads what is on your screen. It never clicks, types or sends anything to the game, and it never touches the game's files. For each shop it reads it sends the item names, prices, quantities, the shop owner's in-game name as shown in the Free Market, the channel and room, the time, and the text of the item tooltip when you hover an item (its stats). From trade chat it sends the message and the poster's in-game name. Club members' watchers can also send small image crops of shop rows for item icons and, when the "sell hover" setting is on, the names and prices of items in their own inventory for the Sell My Items tool; both are off for everyone else. Nothing else on your screen leaves your computer. The watcher keeps a copy of everything it sent in its own folder so you can check.

Scans you contribute become part of the site's price data under section 4. You keep a copy on your own PC and may do what you like with it.

6. Privacy

Browsing needs no account and the site sets no cookies of its own. To count visits, each page view sends the page, the site you came from and a scrambled ID that changes every day; your IP address is not stored. Your choices, such as your watchlist, sell list, positions and theme, are kept in your own browser and never sent to us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary server logs. Seller names shown are in-game character names as anyone standing in the Free Market could see them. Streamers who would rather not be included can tell us through the Feedback button and their channel is skipped from then on. Loading a page also fetches fonts from Google Fonts, item icons and item data from maplestory.io, and price data from GitHub, so those services see your browser's ordinary request (your address and the page you asked for), under their own privacy policies.

Messages sent through the Feedback button go to a private inbox and are read by a person.

7. Donations

Club FM is free and will stay free. Donations through Ko-fi are gifts toward running costs. They buy nothing: no access, no data, no features, no advantage in the game.

8. Enforcement and changes

We may block or limit access for anyone who breaks these terms. These terms may change, and the date at the top shows the latest version. Using the site after a change means you accept the new terms.

Questions or requests: use the Feedback button on the site.
