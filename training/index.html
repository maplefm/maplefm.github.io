Potions and money

  • NPC HP food costs half a meso per HP: Apple, Egg, Red Potion, Fried Chicken and Meat. MP costs one meso per MP: Orange and Lemon.
  • Cake restores 100 HP and 100 MP for 150 mesos, the same as Red Potions and Oranges. Only the 24 Hr Mobile Store in Ant Tunnel Park sells it.
  • White Potions cost 0.7 mesos per HP and Blue Potions 1.1 per MP.
  • Citizenship from level 12 (Henesys or Kerning City) opens the town hall shops: Supreme Sniper Potions in Henesys, Wolbi Throwing Stars in Kerning City.
  • Magicians: 3 points in Magic Guard unlock Magic Armor.
  • Ice-weak: Fire Boar, Copper Drake, Red Drake, Tauromacis. Ice-resistant: Ligator, Cold Eye, Drake, Croco. Lightning-weak: Iron Hog, Lorang, Clang.

Party play

  • Kerning PQ: level 21+, 30 minutes. Stages 2 to 4 need three members on the right spots while the leader talks to Cloto, so bring four.
  • Party EXP: each kill's EXP is divided among the party. The wiki lists no party bonus.
