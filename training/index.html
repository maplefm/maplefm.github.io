Potions and money

  • HP costs about half a meso per HP: Meat and Red Potions are the best value. MP costs about one meso per MP: Lemons, or Pure Water bought on the Free Market.
  • White and Blue Potions are poor value. Unagi, Pure Water and Elixirs are often cheaper on the Free Market than at the NPC.
  • Do every quest: together they hand out a lot of potions and about 212K mesos.
  • Magicians: skip Magic Guard; refilling MP costs more than refilling HP.
  • Dying costs 10% of your level's EXP. There are no EXP events in Classic World.

Party play

  • Kerning PQ: level 21+, no upper limit in Classic, party of 4, 30 minutes, three players can finish. Most players say it beats grinding until 30 to 35.
  • Party EXP bonus is reported as 10%, 20% and 30% for 2, 3 and 4+ members (test server figures; not confirmed for launch).
  • Mesos are split in a party, not increased.
