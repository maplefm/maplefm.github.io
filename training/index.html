Potions and money

  • HP costs about half a meso per HP: Meat and Red Potions are the best value. MP costs about one meso per MP: Lemons, or Pure Water bought on the Free Market.
  • Cake restores 100 HP and 100 MP for 150 mesos: the best NPC item for both.
  • White and Blue Potions are poor value. Unagi, Pure Water and Elixirs are often cheaper on the Free Market than at the NPC.
  • Citizenship from level 12 (Henesys or Kerning): 5% to 25% off shops and taxis, plus Supreme Sniper Potions or Wolbis.
  • Monsters at 10 to 30 drop more potions in Classic.
  • Magicians: put 3 points in Magic Guard to unlock Magic Armor. Keep it off while grinding, on for bosses and Wraiths.
  • Ice-weak: Fire Boar, Copper Drake, Red Drake, Tauromacis. Ice-resistant: Ligator, Cold Eye, Drake, Croco. Lightning-weak: Iron Hog, Lorang, Clang.
  • Most mid-level monsters give 5 to 10% less EXP than old GMS and have a little more avoid.

Party play

  • Kerning PQ: level 21+, no upper limit in Classic, party of 4, 30 minutes, three players can finish. Most guides say party grinding gives more EXP.
  • Party EXP bonus: sources disagree. Reported from +10% for 2 members up to 30% or more for 4 to 6.
  • To share EXP you must be on the map and have attacked, been hit or used a skill in the last 10 seconds.
  • Mesos are split in a party, not increased.
