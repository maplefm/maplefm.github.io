Where to train from level 1 to 10 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every job · updated 2026-10-29

Do the Maple Island quests before you sail: together they pay more than the 300 mesos Shanks charges for the boat to Lith Harbor.

Players recommend

L Forest I

Victoria Island · nearest town Kerning City · 73 spawn points · slow respawns: Iron Hog every 600 s

Ribbon Pigs (21 of 73 spawn points) with snails, pigs and mushrooms. One level 28 Iron Hog also spawns here.
