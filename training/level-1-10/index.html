Training

Where to train from level 1 to 10 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every class · updated 2026-10-25

Do every Maple Island quest first. You leave around level 8 with free potions and a chair.
Warriors can basic attack all the way here and spend almost nothing on potions.
