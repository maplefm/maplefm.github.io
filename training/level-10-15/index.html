Where to train from level 10 to 15 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every job · updated 2026-10-30

Pick maps where you kill in one or two hits; extra hits are where potions go.

Players recommend

Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North IV

Victoria Island · nearest town Ellinia · 44 spawn points

Green Mushrooms and Slimes in the Ellinia tree dungeons. Green Mushrooms drop 28 to 35 mesos on about 68% of kills (wiki, 620 kills).

Touch from Green Mushroom: about 64 damage at level 12 in typical gear, hits 100% of the time, about 22 mesos of Red Potion each.
