Where to train from level 10 to 15 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every class · updated 2026-10-26

Pick maps where you kill in one or two hits; extra hits are where potions go.

What players recommend

Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North IV

Victoria Island · nearest town Ellinia · 44 monsters · 11.0 EXP per 100 HP

Green Mushrooms and Slimes in the Ellinia tree dungeons. Players report coming out meso positive.
