Where to train from level 15 to 21 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every class · updated 2026-10-26

Stay off Dangerous Croko I and II: level 52 Crocos walk through them.

What players recommend

Transfer Area

Victoria Island · nearest town Kerning City · 38 monsters · 12.5 EXP per 100 HP

Stirges from about 20, for magicians, thieves and bowmen. Warriors usually skip Stirges. Crowded. Best for: Magician, Thief, Bowman.

Stirge ×38Lv 20 · 296 HP · 37 EXP

Touch from Stirge: about 101 damage to a level 18 bowman in typical gear, hits 100% of the time, about 35 mesos of Red Potion each. Varies with your gear.

Dangerous Steam

Victoria Island · nearest town Sleepywood · 49 monsters · 11.8 EXP per 100 HP

More Stirges, same advice. Best for: Magician, Thief, Bowman.

Touch from Zombie Mushroom: about 148 damage to a level 18 bowman in typical gear, hits 100% of the time, about 52 mesos of Red Potion each. Varies with your gear.

Damp Forest

Victoria Island · nearest town Sleepywood · 28 monsters · 13.1 EXP per 100 HP

Jr. Neckis from 21, called the safest Necki map. A common pick for mesos and EXP until 30 to 35.
