What players recommend

Transfer Area

Victoria Island · nearest town Kerning City · 38 monsters · 12.5 EXP per 100 HP

Stirges from about 20, for magicians, thieves and bowmen. Warriors usually skip Stirges. Crowded. Best for: Magician, Thief, Bowman.

Stirge ×38Lv 20 · 296 HP · 37 EXP

A touch from Stirge hits a level 18 bowman in typical gear for about 101, landing 100% of the time: about 44 mesos of Red Potion each time. Your own gear changes it.
