Where to train from level 21 to 30 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every class · updated 2026-10-29

Kerning PQ (First Time Together): level 21+, 30 minutes. A full run gives 1,234 EXP at level 21 and 3,000 from level 30.
Iron Hogs have 500 weapon defense but 100 magic defense, and are weak to lightning.
