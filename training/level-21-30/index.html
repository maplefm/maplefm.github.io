Where to train from level 21 to 30 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every class · updated 2026-10-28

Kerning PQ (21+, party of 4) is fun, but most guides say party grinding gives more EXP: about half a level a run at 21 to 22.
Iron Hogs: 500 physical defense, so physical attacks do about a sixth of normal damage. Mages with lightning do best.
Melee profit in the Ant Tunnels is disputed: several warriors said it was their first meso-negative map; ranged classes report profit.
