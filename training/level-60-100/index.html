Where to train from level 60 to 100 in MapleStory Classic

Training guide · every class · updated 2026-10-28

Several maps here respawn slowly (wiki): Wild Kargos and Tauromacis every 120 s, Red Drakes every 20 to 60 s.

Our picks

Wild Kargo's Area

Victoria Island · nearest town Sleepywood · 31 spawn points · 4.5 EXP per 100 HP · slow respawns: Wild Kargo every 120 s

14 Wild Kargo spawn points (level 62) among 17 Cold Eyes. About 117 accuracy hits every time at level 62, 135 at 58.

Cold Eye ×17Lv 40 · 1,894 HP · 85 EXPWild Kargo ×14Lv 62 · 5,057 HP · 231 EXP

Touch from Wild Kargo: about 349 damage to a level 67 bowman in typical gear, hits 100% of the time, about 122 mesos of Red Potion each. Varies with your gear.

Dangerous Valley II

Victoria Island · nearest town Perion · 30 spawn points · 4.3 EXP per 100 HP · slow respawns: Red Drake every 37 s

17 Red Drake spawn points (level 60) with Copper Drakes and Fire Boars. Red Drakes are weak to ice.

Red Drake ×17Lv 60 · 5,184 HP · 211 EXPFire Boar ×8Lv 32 · 842 HP · 60 EXPCopper Drake ×5Lv 45 · 2,595 HP · 107 EXP

Touch from Red Drake: about 338 damage to a level 67 bowman in typical gear, hits 100% of the time, about 118 mesos of Red Potion each. Varies with your gear.

Sanctuary Entrance I

Victoria Island · nearest town Sleepywood · 17 spawn points · 4.0 EXP per 100 HP · slow respawns: Tauromacis every 120 s

4 Tauromacis (level 70) among 13 Cold Eyes. Taurospears start at Sanctuary Entrance III.

Cold Eye ×13Lv 40 · 1,894 HP · 85 EXPTauromacis ×4Lv 70 · 9,328 HP · 337 EXP

Touch from Tauromacis: about 438 damage to a level 67 bowman in typical gear, hits 100% of the time, about 153 mesos of Red Potion each. Varies with your gear.
